Terms & Refund Policy

Lama Topup · Free Fire & eFootball top up, Nepal

1. How it works

Choose a package, enter your game ID, pay by Fonepay QR (eSewa, Khalti or mobile banking), and enter your transaction ID. We check the payment and then deliver your top up. Delivery is done by hand, so it can take a little time, especially at night.

2. Your game ID

  • Please double-check your Player ID / UID before paying. The name shown on our site is only a convenience check and may not always be available.
  • A top up delivered to the ID you entered cannot be reversed or refunded, even if the ID was typed wrongly.
  • We never ask for your password. Do not share it with anyone.

3. Payment

  • Pay the exact amount shown for your package.
  • Each transaction ID can be used for one order only. Orders that use a reused or invalid transaction ID will be rejected.

4. Refunds

  • If we cannot deliver your order, or your order is cancelled after you have paid, we refund the amount to the account you paid from after we verify the payment.
  • To request a refund, message us on WhatsApp with your order ID and transaction ID.
  • Orders that have been delivered to the ID you provided are not refundable.

5. Memberships

Weekly and Monthly Memberships start from the delivery date. An ID that already has an active membership may not be able to receive a new one until it ends.
